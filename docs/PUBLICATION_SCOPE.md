# Public publication scope

Published by the user's instruction: code, design memos, and engineering lineage. Historical account chat excerpts and exported conversation collections are excluded. The earlier full recovery master and full transfer ZIP are not public release assets.

The runtime and modular candidates preserve their original source bytes, license/provenance notices, and synthetic test fixtures. Original engineering test/review claims remain attributed; publication does not repair or accept M1.1. General machine path labels in the newly compiled level map are normalized to artifact filenames.

The public engineering index is regenerated from the files in this public tree. It is not copied from the broad private recovery index. Hashes identify artifacts; their presence does not prove execution, completion, authority, or global agreement.

The public source text and ZIP can reconstruct the published tree. The private historical master remains separate. New documentation, utilities, and presentation are licensed only as specified in LICENSES.md; inherited material retains its own terms.
